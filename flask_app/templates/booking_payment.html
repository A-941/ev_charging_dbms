How Reservation Works
  • 1
    Direct Customer Entry: Type driver name and mobile number. Existing drivers are seamlessly linked; new drivers are automatically created.
  • 2
    Automatic Port Lock: Once confirmed, SQLite trigger trg_port_reserved_on_booking prevents double bookings.
  • 3
    Seamless Handoff: When charging finishes, proceed to Step 2 to enter kWh energy and settle the bill in INR (₹).
Tariff & Taxes in India

Tariffs range between ₹12.00 - ₹24.00 per kWh depending on charger speed (Type 2 AC vs. CCS2 120kW DC Fast). A standard ₹25 base connection charge and 5% statutory electricity GST are computed at settlement.
